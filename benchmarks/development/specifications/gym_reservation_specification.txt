
FitHub's booking page lets people book a single class. They first say who they are: a drop-in visitor, a member, or a corporate member through their employer. They then choose a class (yoga, spin, bootcamp, HIIT, running club or pilates), a time slot (morning, midday or evening) and a trainer (Ana, Ben, Chloe, Dev or Ewa). Each booking also records whether the class takes place outdoors or in the studio.

Five extras are each yes or no: a free trial class, a locker, equipment hire, a text reminder, and adding the class to their calendar.

Some combinations are not possible:

- Free trial classes are only for drop-in visitors.
- Lockers are for members and corporate members only, not for drop-in visitors.
- Yoga, spin and pilates are always in the studio, and running club is always outdoors.
- Bootcamp and HIIT move outdoors for the morning slot, and are held in the studio at midday and in the evening.

Running club meets at every time slot, and any trainer can teach any class. The page also shows how many places are left, a waiting-list button, and a map of the gym; none of these are booking choices.
