

At TastyPizza & Bros. customers build one pizza at a time by choosing a crust (party sheet, classic, thin, stuffed or gluten-free) and a size (small, medium, large or family), where party sheet comes only in family size and is the only crust made in family size, classic comes in small, medium and large, thin and gluten-free come only in small and large, and stuffed comes only in medium; they then pick a sauce (truffle cream, tomato, BBQ, garlic, pesto or spicy), a cheese (burrata, mozzarella, cheddar or vegan) and a bake (normal, light or well done), and decide whether to add mushrooms, olives, peppers, extra cheese and double meat, each of which can be added or left off; four of these choices, namely extra cheese, double meat, truffle cream sauce and burrata cheese, are premium upgrades, and a pizza can have at most two of them, any other combination being allowed, while the price, the note about oven temperature and the allergy notice on the menu are not choices.
