ABC travels sells flight-and-hotel trips from UK airports, and on the trip page a customer chooses a departure airport (London, Manchester, Birmingham, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Bristol, Newcastle, Liverpool, Leeds, Belfast, Cardiff, Aberdeen or Southampton), a destination (Lisbon, Porto, Madrid, Barcelona, Malaga, Seville, Paris, Nice, Rome, Milan, Naples, Venice, Athens, Dubrovnik, Split, Prague or Vienna), a departure day of the month from the 1st to the 31st (the month itself is picked on an earlier screen that is not part of this page, so every day from 1 to 31 is always offered), a stay of 3 to 13 nights, a party of 1 to 6 travellers, between none and 5 checked bags for the whole booking, a cabin class of economy, premium economy or business, and a meal preference of standard, vegetarian, vegan, halal or gluten-free, and then ticks or leaves unticked each of six add-ons, namely travel insurance, seat selection, an airport transfer at the destination, a flexible ticket, priority boarding and carbon offsetting; all of these choices are independent, so any combination can be booked, while the live prices and the newsletter tick box shown on the page are not part of the trip.
