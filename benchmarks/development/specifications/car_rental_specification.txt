# DriveAway online car rental

When booking a rental car through the DriveAway website, the customer selects a car size (compact, midsize or SUV), a rental length (one day, a weekend, a week, a fortnight or a month), a pickup time (morning, afternoon or evening), a fuel policy (full-to-full, prepaid tank, or pay per litre used) and a level of cover (basic, standard or premium), and says whether to add a satnav and whether to add a child seat; the customer also indicates whether the booking is made with a DriveAway member account or as a guest, whether the booking includes free cancellation, and whether payment will be made at pickup or prepaid online. Free cancellation is a member benefit: every member booking includes it, and guest bookings cannot include it. Guest bookings must be prepaid online, and one-month rentals must also be prepaid online, even when booked by a member, while members may pay at pickup for all shorter rentals, including a fortnight. Any combination not restricted by these rules is permitted, and the daily price and the rental counter's opening hours shown on the booking page are for information only and are not booking options.
